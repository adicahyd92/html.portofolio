About Me

Strategic Learning & Development Professional | Instructional Designer | EdTech Specialist

I am a dedicated Training & Development specialist with over 8 years of experience designing, delivering, and scaling impactful learning solutions across healthcare, retail, and corporate sectors. My expertise spans the full instructional design lifecycle—from conducting deep Training Needs Analysis (TNA) to creating engaging hybrid curriculum assets and managing complex Learning Management Systems (LMS) like Moodle.

Driven by a passion for leveraging technology to transform education, I am currently expanding my technical and pedagogical toolsets through a Graduate Certificate in Education (Digital Learning) at Adelaide University, alongside hands-on Web Development training. In my current role as Online Learning Administrator at Deaf Connect, I combine LMS administration, digital content curation, and data tracking to deliver seamless, accessible learning experiences.
